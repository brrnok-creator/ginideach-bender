GINIDEACH BENDER — 100 ROUND COMIC ADVENTURE

Upload all files in this folder to the root of your GitHub Pages repository. The existing website address remains the same. The game uses 21 new illustrated scene crops adapted from the approved adventure storyboard, plus the original pub and bonus cartoons. Travel (51–79) rotates through six scenes; enlightenment (80–99) rotates through six scenes. Milestone scenes cover 20, 30, 40, 41–49 and 50. At 100 the game ends with the heavenly celebration and the exact message: Ginideach Bender na Neimhe bainte amach.

Features preserved: three grammatical modes, random gender bonuses, incorrect-answer strikethrough, streak and record, 12 irregular nouns in the first 30 main questions, example sentences, and email comments to richsideshow@gmail.com (with noun and question prefilled).

Notes: storyboard crops are reused across ranges rather than 100 individually unique cartoons. Some artwork panels have smaller aspect ratios and are enlarged for phone display. The gender/genitive plural forms come from BuNaMo and are not individually verified against Teanglann. Feedback uses the device email app; it does not submit automatically.

To update a previously installed phone app, refresh in Chrome or fully close and reopen it after the GitHub Pages update. The service-worker cache version has been changed.
